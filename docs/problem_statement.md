# Problem Statement

## What problem are we solving?

Booking flights is often confusing because prices change frequently, users must compare many options, and there is little guidance on whether to book immediately or wait. Most flight booking platforms focus on displaying available flights rather than helping users make informed decisions.

Travelers also struggle to identify the best flight based on multiple factors such as price, travel time, number of stops, airline preference, and historical price trends. As a result, users spend significant time comparing options and may still make poor booking decisions.

## Who are the users?

- Individual travelers
- Business travelers
- Students
- Families planning vacations
- Frequent flyers
- Travel enthusiasts

## What are the users' pain points?

- Flight prices change unpredictably.
- Users do not know the best time to book.
- Comparing hundreds of flights is time-consuming.
- Existing platforms prioritize price instead of overall value.
- Users receive little personalization.
- There is limited insight into travel trends and historical pricing.

## What solution will the platform provide?

The AI Flight Intelligence Platform combines flight search with artificial intelligence to help users make smarter travel decisions. The platform enables users to search flights, predict future prices, receive personalized recommendations, analyze travel trends, and interact with the system using natural language.

Machine learning models estimate future flight prices, while recommendation algorithms rank flights based on user preferences rather than price alone. Interactive dashboards provide useful travel analytics for both users and administrators.

## What makes this project different?

Unlike traditional flight booking websites, this platform focuses on decision intelligence rather than simple flight search. It integrates machine learning, recommendation systems, natural language processing, and analytics into a single application. The project demonstrates practical AI engineering by combining data science, machine learning, and full-stack development in a real-world travel domain.
