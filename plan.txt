## Overall recommendation

At this point, I would **freeze the project scope**. Resist the temptation to add features like notifications, Outlook support, team workspaces, advanced analytics, or autonomous sending. Your current trajectory is well aligned with what interviewers expect from a strong AI/backend portfolio project: a focused application with a complete, explainable end-to-end workflow rather than a sprawling feature list. Finish this roadmap before considering any enhancements.
